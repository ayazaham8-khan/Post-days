❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How do I install the app on my phone?
Open the app in Chrome, tap the 3-dot menu (⋮), and select "Add to Home Screen". It will install like a native app with an icon on your home screen.
Does the app work without internet?
Yes! After your first visit, the app caches all content and works fully offline. Perfect for travel or poor connectivity.
Can I suggest a missing international day?
Absolutely! Send us an email at ayazaham8@gmail.com with the event name, date, and description. We review all suggestions.
Is the app free to use?
The core app is free. We're working on a Pro tier with advanced creator features like unlimited hooks, captions, and AI-generated content.
How do I report incorrect event information?
Use the form above with subject "Missing Event / Correction" and we will verify and update the information as quickly as possible.
