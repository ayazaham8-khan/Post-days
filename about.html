Our Story
Why We Built This
Content creators, social media managers, and educators often struggle with one question: "What should I post today?"

We realized that every single day has internationally recognized significance — from World Health Day to International Women's Day to World Chocolate Day. Yet most people never know about them until it is too late to create timely content.

Global Days Tracker was built to solve this problem. We curated 200+ international observance days, added creator-ready hooks and captions, and wrapped it all in a beautiful, fast mobile app that works even offline.

Our Mission
Making Every Day Count
We believe that awareness creates change. When more people know about World Mental Health Day, more conversations happen. When creators post about World Environment Day, more people take action.

Our mission is to make global awareness days accessible to everyone — from professional content creators to curious students — and to help translate awareness into action and content.
